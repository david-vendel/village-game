# Current problems

From the first screenshot (`screenshots/20261003-1151.webp`, the farm as the game had it), worst
first:

1. **The roof shape is wrong.**
   - It has a large cross gable over the right end and a small dormer. The reference has one long
     gable roof and one tall, pointed dormer.
   - The roof reads as corrugated sheet, not hand-made tiles.
   - It has no flare and almost no overhang.
2. **The great chimney is missing.** The reference's main feature is a massive grey stone stack on
   the right gable, from the ground to above the ridge. Ours is a small chimney on the left of the
   roof.
3. **Three arches instead of two.** All three are the same. The reference has a wide arch with
   doors and a narrower open one.
4. **The jetty is too shallow,** with no visible joist ends or brackets.
5. **Too many timbers.** Braces in every bay and shutters on every window make it busy. The
   reference's frame is calmer, with thicker, darker timbers.
6. **Everything is ruler straight:** no sag, no crooked timbers, no irregular stone.
7. **The greenery is sparse and blocky:** single patches instead of ivy masses, and no shrubs.
8. **Props:** there are no hay bales or crates before the barn, and the cart stands in front of the
   right arch.
9. **Missing textures:** rubble (chimney), earth (yard), leaves.
