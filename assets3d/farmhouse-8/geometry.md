# Geometry rules: farmhouse #8

Measures are in metres. The plot is 7.5 wide (x) by 3.75 deep (y), with the front at y0.

## Proportions (from the reference, front view)

| Part | Share of the height to the ridge |
| --- | --- |
| Stone barn storey | about 40 % |
| Jettied timber storey | about 28 % |
| Roof | about 32 % (steep, about 50°) |

The chimney stands about 1 m above the ridge.

## Rules
1. **Barn wall:** coursed ashlar.
   - Course heights 0.22–0.30, blocks 0.4–0.8 long, joints staggered.
   - Corner quoins a little larger.
2. **Arches:** two, not three. Voussoirs are about 0.3 deep, with the keystone a little proud.
   - **Left arch:** about 2.2 wide, semicircular, with a pair of plank leaves.
   - **Right arch:** about 1.5 wide, taller in proportion, a dark passage. The farmer's door at
     `HOME.dx` must stay a door that opens, so this arch gets a door set back in the passage.
3. **Jetty:** the upper floor stands out 0.45–0.6 over the front.
   - It rests on joist ends spaced about 0.5 apart.
   - Curved brackets sit under the corners and between the arches.
4. **Upper frame:**
   - Posts about 1.2 apart, a sill beam, a head beam, and one rail at window-sill height.
   - Braces only in the end bays, plus one by the dormer.
   - Timbers 0.18–0.22 thick and slightly crooked (a few cm), not ruler straight.
5. **Windows:**
   - Three on the front of the upper floor, small (0.55 × 0.65), with leaded panes and window
     boxes of trailing plants.
   - One on the right side wall.
6. **Roof:** a single gable along the street, with no cross gable.
   - Eaves overhang 0.5 at the front and 0.35 at the gables.
   - A slight flare (bellcast) at the eaves.
   - The ridge sags a little (a few cm).
7. **Dormer:** one, left of the middle, steep and pointed. It has its own tiled roof and a
   two-light window.
8. **Chimney:** a stack of rough grey fieldstone against the right gable, rising from the ground.
   - About 1.1 × 0.8 at the base, stepping in to 0.8 × 0.6 above the eaves.
   - A stone cap and a pot.
   - It must stay within the plot.
9. **Greenery and props:**
   - Ivy in masses, not single leaves, up the right corner and the chimney.
   - Cart, bales, barrel and crates inside the plot, clear of the doors and the grain store.
