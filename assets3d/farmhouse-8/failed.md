# Failed experiments

From earlier work on the same goal (see `.art-raw/flux/style/REPORT.md` and
`.art-raw/trellis/REPORT.md`, local):

- **TRELLIS.2 image-to-3D from #8** (2026-10-02/03): one fused, blobby mesh with smeared sides and
  an invented back, and no stages, doors or footprint. Not usable as game geometry.
- **FLUX cut-outs as 2D buildings** (#4–#8): they look good from one angle, but they are flat
  pictures with hazy blue backgrounds, no stages and no night.
- **SDXL paint-over of the 3D model** (#9): keeps the shape but repaints it loosely, so the result
  drifts from the model and from frame to frame.
- **Painted textures alone (step 1):** they improved the thatch and the stone, but changed the
  timber and plaster little. Textures can't fix wrong shapes.
